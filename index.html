Safety Tips

Simple habits can make emergency situations easier to handle.

01 — Stay aware
Keep your surroundings in mind, especially when travelling alone.
02 — Keep contacts ready
Add people you trust before you actually need emergency assistance.
03 — Share your location carefully
Only share your location with trusted people or emergency services.
04 — Know emergency numbers
India's emergency response number is 112.
